Retinal Fundus Multi-Disease Image Classification using Hybrid CNN-Transformer-Ensemble Architectures
Saksham Agarwal, Deependra Singh, et al.
We develop a comprehensive diagnostic system capable of accurately classifying 20 retinal disease labels solely from fundus images. Our approach introduces hybrid models combining deeper CNNs, Transformer encoders, and ensemble architectures arranged sequentially and in parallel. We overcome critical challenges of limited, diverse datasets and imbalanced class distributions through novel data augmentation and synthetic oversampling strategies. The system bridges a significant healthcare gap by providing diagnostic capability in regions underserved by specialized ophthalmological expertise.

Interpretable AI Models for Detecting and Classifying Multiple Retinal Conditions
Saksham Agarwal, Deependra Singh, et al.
An extension of our retinal disease classification framework with a strong focus on explainability and clinical interpretability. We incorporate gradient-based attribution (GradCAM, Integrated Gradients) and attention visualization techniques to enable clinicians to understand and trust model predictions. The system targets multi-label classification across 20 retinal conditions, with interpretability modules designed to highlight pathologically relevant image regions consistent with ophthalmological expertise.

HetGSMOTE: Addressing Class Imbalance in Heterogeneous Graph Neural Networks
Deependra Singh, et al.
We propose HetGSMOTE (Heterogeneous Graph SMOTE), a novel oversampling method for heterogeneous graph-structured data. By generating synthetic minority-class nodes in the latent space of a heterogeneous graph neural network, our method significantly improves classification performance on imbalanced heterogeneous graphs. We demonstrate consistent improvements over existing baselines on multiple benchmark datasets, offering a principled approach to class imbalance in relational learning.

Proton Pressure in Terms of Degenerate Quark Distribution
D.R.J. Marattukalam, T.Z. Win, C.W. Aung, S. Ghosh, Deependra Singh (acknowledged)
We map the experimentally measured pressure distribution inside the proton (extracted via Deeply Virtual Compton Scattering, peak ~10³⁵ Pa at 0.2 fm) using a degenerate quark distribution model. A radial-dependent chemical potential effectively encodes the complex three-body quark potential and surrounding sea quarks, reproducing the strongly repulsive core and confining tail observed in experiment. Work carried out during summer fellowship at IIT Bhilai.
