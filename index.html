About

Probing the Foundations of Reality

I'm a PhD Research Scholar at the Harish-Chandra Research Institute (HRI), Prayagraj, where I work at the intersection of Quantum Information Theory and Quantum Foundations. My research explores entanglement theory, collapse models, quantum reference frames, and the deep mathematical structure underlying quantum mechanics.

I completed my Integrated M.Sc. in Physics at NISER Bhubaneswar (2020–2025) with a GPA of 8.58/10 and a minor in Computer Science. My master's thesis studied the quantization of bosonic strings in flat spacetime through conformal field theory and representation theory. Along the way I've worked on ML (deep learning, graph neural networks), computational physics (Monte Carlo, Feynman integrals), and quantum computing (QKD protocols via Qiskit).
