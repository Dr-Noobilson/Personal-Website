QKD Protocols
Full simulation of the BB84 and E91 Quantum Key Distribution protocols built with Qiskit and Flask. Demonstrates quantum cryptography over a simulated multi-device network, showcasing how quantum principles break classical encryption assumptions (Shor's algorithm vs RSA).
Qiskit Quantum Crypto Python

Double Exchange Model
Monte Carlo simulation of the Double Exchange Model in the limit of large Hund's coupling, a key model for understanding colossal magnetoresistance in manganites. Explores phase transitions between ferromagnetic metallic and antiferromagnetic insulating phases.
Monte Carlo Condensed Matter Python

VMC Importance Sampling
Variational Monte Carlo framework with importance sampling for estimating quantum ground-state energies. Implements the Metropolis-Hastings algorithm with trial wave functions, demonstrating the power of stochastic methods for many-body quantum systems.
Variational MC Quantum Many-Body Python / NumPy

Retinal Disease Classifier
Hybrid CNN-Transformer-Ensemble architecture for multi-label classification of 20 retinal diseases from fundus images. Addressed severe class imbalance with novel augmentation. Published at IHIC 2023 (Springer LNNS). Extended with explainability techniques for ACM TCH submission.

HetGSMOTE
Novel oversampling technique for heterogeneous graph neural networks. Generates synthetic minority-class node embeddings in the GNN latent space to address class imbalance in relational learning tasks. Submitted to NLDL 2026.
